AAbout

Why a jar full of jargon?

01The story

I’ve often been taken for a junior. Not because of my work, but because of my vocabulary. I care about what a pattern does more than what it’s called, so I forget the names. People’s attitude changed fast once they saw my deliverables, but I’d rather not need that second chance.

That’s why I built Jargon Jar. Credibility can slip quickly when the right word doesn’t come, even if you know exactly what you’re doing. And with AI, the stakes are higher: the more precise your terms, the better and faster your prompts perform. I’m in love with AI, and my goal is to get the most out of it in my field.

I use the quiz every day to build my own vocabulary. It gives you a level, the right answers and links to go deeper. It’s a quick check, not a certification.

02Who writes the questions

Clara Boustany Le Roux, UX/UI and AI designer. 7 years of practice, including Pictet Asset Management (design system foundations and an internal app redesign) and ISO. Independent from November 2026, designing with AI for the past year.

03How questions are written and checked

  1. Each term comes from real project work: critiques, handoffs, research readouts and AI feature specs.
  2. Every question has one clearly right answer and three plausible distractors, never trick wording.
  3. Explanations are checked against primary sources, such as WCAG, Nielsen Norman Group and model provider docs.
  4. Difficulty is tagged easy, medium or hard and reviewed after real people take the quiz.
  5. Spot an error? Report it on the Contact page and it gets fixed.
