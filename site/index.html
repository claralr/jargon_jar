01How it works

Three minutes, three steps.

No sign-up. Your answers stay in your browser.

  1. Step 01 · about 3 min

    Answer

    12 questions on UX, UI, AI or a mix, 3 per level. Easy terms first, trickier ones later.

  2. Step 02 · instant

    Get your level

    Harder questions count more. You see your level, your score per area and how it was worked out.

  3. Step 03 · whenever you like

    Learn the gaps

    Every answer is explained. Your weakest area comes with three short reads.

02The levels

Four levels, from Curious to Expert.

Your level comes from your weighted score. It tells you where you stand today, not who you are.

  • Level 01/040–44% of points

    Curious

    You know many of the words exist. You’re starting to connect them to real work.

  • Level 02/0445–69% of points

    Practitioner

    You use the core terms correctly every day. Some specialist terms still slip.

  • Level 03/0470–87% of points

    Specialist

    You know the nuances and can explain them to your team.

  • Level 04/0488–100% of points

    Expert

    You spot misuse, teach the terms and can argue the edge cases.
